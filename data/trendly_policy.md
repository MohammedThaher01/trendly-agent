# Autonomous Support Agent — Shipping & Returns Reference

*Effective 1 January 2026. Authoritative rule set for customer logic evaluation.*

---

## 1. Shipping

**1.1 Dispatch times.** Orders placed before 2:00 PM IST on a business day dispatch the same day. Orders placed after 2:00 PM IST, on weekends, or on public holidays dispatch the next business day.

**1.2 Delivery estimates.** Metro cities: 2–4 business days. Non-metro cities: 4–7 business days. Remote pincodes: up to 10 business days.

**1.3 Shipping charges.** Free standard shipping on all orders of ₹1,499 and above. Orders below ₹1,499 carry a flat ₹99 shipping fee. Express shipping is ₹199 flat and is unavailable for cash-on-delivery orders.

**1.4 Partial shipments.** When an order contains a backordered item, available items ship first at no extra cost. The remaining items ship when back in stock.

**1.5 Delayed orders.** An order is considered delayed once it is more than 3 business days past its expected delivery date. Delayed orders qualify for a ₹250 store credit on request.

**1.6 Lost parcels.** If a carrier marks a parcel lost, or if a parcel shows no tracking movement for 10 consecutive days, it is treated as a **lost-parcel claim, not a return**. These require human agent escalation and are resolved within 5 business days by replacement or full refund.

**1.7 Address changes.** Delivery addresses can be changed only before dispatch.

---

## 2. Returns

**2.1 Return window.** Items may be returned within **30 calendar days of the delivery date**. Requests made after 30 days are ineligible.

**2.2 Condition.** Items must be unworn, unwashed, and returned with original tags attached and original packaging.

**2.3 Non-returnable categories.** The following cannot be returned or exchanged for hygiene and safety reasons:
- Innerwear and socks
- Jewellery
- Beauty and fragrance products
- Face masks
- Gift cards

**2.4 Final sale items.** Items marked **final sale** are eligible for **size exchange only** — no refunds and no store credit.

**2.5 Footwear.** Footwear is returnable in its original shoe box. Returns without the box incur a ₹300 deduction.

**2.6 Cancelled orders.** Once an order is cancelled, no return can be raised against it.

---

## 3. Refunds

**3.1 Timelines.** After a returned item passes warehouse inspection (2–3 business days):
- Credit / debit card: 5–7 business days
- UPI: 3–5 business days
- Cash on delivery: Bank transfer or store credit (7–10 business days)
- Store credit: Immediate

**3.2 Shipping fees.** The ₹99 shipping fee is refunded only if the return is due to an error (wrong, damaged, or defective item).

**3.3 Cash-on-delivery refunds.** Require bank account details collected by a human agent over a secure link. The assistant must never collect bank details in chat.

**3.4 Partial refunds.** If only some items are returned, only those items are refunded.

---

## 4. Exchanges

**4.1 Scope.** Size exchanges only — not colour or style exchanges.

**4.2 Window.** Same 30-day window.

**4.3 Availability.** If the requested size is unavailable, the exchange converts to a refund under section 3.

**4.4 Limit.** One exchange per item.

---

## 5. Return pickup

**5.1 Pickup.** Free reverse pickup is available on all serviceable pincodes (up to 2 attempts).

**5.2 Non-serviceable pincodes.** Customer self-ships and is reimbursed up to ₹150 against a receipt.

---

## 6. Damaged or wrong items

**6.1 Reporting window.** Must be reported within **48 hours of delivery**, with photographs.

**6.2 Resolution.** Replacement at no cost or full refund including shipping, at customer choice.

---

## 7. What the assistant must not do

- Offer unapproved discounts, coupons, waivers, or goodwill credits
- Collect bank account numbers, card numbers, or CVV in chat
- Give medical, legal, or financial advice
- Confirm or discuss orders belonging to a different customer
- Invent policy where this document is silent — must state it does not know and offer a human agent
